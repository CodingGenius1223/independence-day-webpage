ACTS OF FREEDOM

Important milestones from India's journey towards independence.

1857

FIRST WAR OF INDEPENDENCE

The Revolt of 1857 was one of the earliest major uprisings against British rule in India.

1885

INDIAN NATIONAL CONGRESS

The Indian National Congress was formed and became an important part of India's freedom movement.

1920

NON-COOPERATION MOVEMENT

Indians joined together to peacefully oppose British rule and institutions.

1930

SALT MARCH

Mahatma Gandhi led the historic Dandi March against the British salt tax.

1942

QUIT INDIA MOVEMENT

The movement demanded an immediate end to British rule in India.

1947

INDIA BECAME INDEPENDENT

India achieved independence on 15 August 1947 after a long struggle for freedom.
